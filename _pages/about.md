Hi! This is Runchu.

I am a first year PhD student in [**Prof. Mohit Bansal's Group**](https://www.cs.unc.edu/~mbansal/) at [**UNC-Chapel Hill**](https://cs.unc.edu/), working on **LLM post-training for long-horizon agentic tasks**.

I received my [**MS**](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/academics/graduate/ms-program) degree from UIUC, where I was advised by Prof. [**Jiawei Han**](http://hanj.cs.illinois.edu/) on **LLM enhanced Information Retrieval**. Before that, I earned my **Bachelor** degree from [**Tsinghua University**](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), where I was advised by Prof. [**Zhiyuan Liu**](https://nlp.csai.tsinghua.edu.cn/~lzy/) on **LLM agents**.

I am actively looking for academic and industrial connections and opportunities. Feel free to contact me for collaborations, connections, or any questions you may have whether by email (runchutian\[at\]gmail.com) or through social media. Thank you!
